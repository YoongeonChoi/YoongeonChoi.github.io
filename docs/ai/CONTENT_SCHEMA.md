# Content Schema

## Project Required Sections
- one-line summary
- problem
- role
- why this stack
- design and implementation
- hard parts
- outcome / metrics
- retrospective
- links

## Article Required Sections
- what this solves
- who this is for
- key takeaway
- body
- conclusion
- related work

## Completeness Scoring
- title: 10
- summary: 10
- body: 20
- cover + alt: 15
- SEO fields: 15
- links/tags: 10
- structured sections present: 20

## Forbidden Content
- unverifiable claims
- fake metrics
- empty marketing filler
