# Editorial Policy

## Tone
- precise
- confident
- no hype
- no vague buzzwords

## Claims
- numbers require source or direct authorship
- security/privacy claims must match implementation

## Translation
- `ko` is source by default
- `en` is source-linked parallel track
- stale translations should not be presented as current

## Contact And Privacy
- do not request unnecessary personal data
- do not expose internal email or secrets
- do not imply guaranteed response outside policy
